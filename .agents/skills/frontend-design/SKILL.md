# Frontend Design

## Ground Designs in Subject Matter
- Build with the subject's real world: Logistics, Supply Chain economics, Digital Ventures, and Full-Stack Engineering.
- Hero must open with the most characteristic thing: bold statement of craft, real interactive product artifacts, and an authentic portrait.
- Avoid accidental tells: single-word gradient clipping, unneeded all-caps labels, and repetitive 3-column feature grids.
